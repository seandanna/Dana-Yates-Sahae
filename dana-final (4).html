Journalist Who Held City Hall Accountable Is Now Running to Represent District 5

She spent her career asking the questions officials didn't want to answer. Now she's running to make sure District 5 residents get the answers they deserve — before the votes are cast, not after.

By Dana Yates Sahae · Candidate for San Mateo City Council District 5
Dana Yates Sahae, lifelong San Mateo resident and former award-winning journalist, running for City Council District 5.

I grew up in San Mateo. My father Gary Yates served seven years on the City Council. From childhood, I watched local government from the inside — and understood that it works best when residents are genuinely heard, not notified after decisions are already made.

I spent years as an award-winning journalist for the San Mateo Daily Journal, covering City Hall, public safety, and small businesses. I broke stories officials didn't want told. I asked questions they hoped would never be asked. That's not a credential I'm leaving behind — it's exactly what I'm bringing to the dais.

"I've never cast a vote residents didn't know was coming. I've never approved a plan without asking who was consulted. That's not inexperience. That's a promise."

— Dana Yates Sahae

District 5 is at a turning point. The Hillsdale redevelopment will transform this neighborhood — and right now, the people making those decisions have more access to the process than the people who live here. That needs to change. And changing it is exactly what journalists do.

The Questions
Question No. 1
Who Was Consulted Before the Bohannon Plan?

The largest development in San Mateo's history is coming to District 5. Thousands of units. Massive traffic impact. Who asked the residents first?

Dana will. Before any vote is cast, not after.
Add Dana's confirmed position on the Bohannon/Hillsdale development.
Question No. 2
Where Is Every Dollar of Your Tax Money Going?

San Mateo faces a structural budget deficit. Services are at risk. The decisions being made now will determine what survives — and nobody's explaining them in plain language.

Dana will ask. And she'll make sure you understand the answer before the vote.
Question No. 3
Why Does City Hall Know Before You Do?

Too many decisions are made before residents know they're being considered. By the time you find out, it's already done. That's not government — it's notification.

Dana will insist on genuine community input before decisions are finalized — not public comment after.
Question No. 4
Who's Fighting for District 5 at the Table?

Development pressure, traffic, budget cuts — District 5 needs a voice that asks the hard questions and doesn't stop until they're answered.

Dana spent her career not stopping until she got the answer. That doesn't change when she joins the Council.

Transparent, Accountable Government

Dana spent her career making sure the public knew what its government was doing. She's not changing careers — she's changing which side of the table she sits on.

  • Plain-language communication: Every major decision explained to residents before it's made.
  • Genuine input: Residents get a seat at the table — not a public comment period after the deal is done.
  • Budget transparency: Every dollar explained and justified. Dana will ask the questions taxpayers deserve answered.
What's really in the Bohannon plan? Dana will find out — and tell you.
🏘️

Smart Growth & Neighborhood Voice

The Hillsdale redevelopment will transform District 5. Dana won't rubber-stamp it. She'll read every page, ask every question, and make sure residents have a genuine voice — not a notification.

  • Community input first: No development plan advances without genuine resident consultation.
  • Infrastructure accountability: Every new unit comes with the roads, parks, and services to support it.
  • Neighborhood character: The reason people chose District 5 is worth protecting.

Public Safety & Fiscal Accountability

San Mateo's budget deficit will require hard choices. Dana will ask the hard questions about every cut — because she's been asking hard questions her entire career, and she's not going to stop now.

  • Honest budget answers: No wishful thinking. A real fiscal plan that residents understand and approve.
